<h2 id="camp">About camp</h2>

Saddle Up campers spend most of each day on their bikes, riding through neighborhoods to parks, trails, and fun destinations. Along the way they practice bike handling and safe street riding, plan routes, learn to take a bike on transit, and pick up basic bike maintenance.

### Camp at a glance

* **Age groups:** Wheelies, ages 8–10; Cruisers, ages 11–12; Counselors in training, ages 13 and up with previous camp experience
* **Schedule:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–3:00 PM. Drop-off is 8:30–9:00 AM and pick-up is 3:00–3:30 PM.
* **Group size:** Up to 15 campers per camp
* **Cost:** $350 per week in 2026. 2027 pricing will be posted when registration opens.
* **Scholarships:** Available. See [Are there scholarships?](#are-there-scholarships) below.

### What campers need

* A working bike **with gears** (no training wheels), and the ability to start, stop, and ride in a straight line. We have some geared bikes to lend for the week; please let us know ahead of time.
* A properly fitted bike helmet
* Closed-toe shoes
* A sack lunch, snacks, and a water bottle each day. We don't provide food.
* Campers who need medication must be able to take it themselves.

<h2 id="registration">How registration works</h2>

Registration for 2027 opens in March. Here's what to expect, so you can plan ahead. Each step is done separately for each camper.

1. **Register.** Fill out the online registration form and upload a head-shot photo for your camper's ID badge. At the end you'll go to the payment page to pay in full or pay a deposit. To apply for a scholarship, choose that option on the form and email [camps@washcobikes.org](mailto:camps@washcobikes.org).
2. **Final payment, due June 1.** If you registered with a deposit, the balance is due June 1, no matter which week your camper attends.
3. **Two weeks before camp.** Complete the online waivers and policies form (signed electronically) and the emergency and photo information form, including everyone authorized to pick up your camper.
4. **One week before camp.** Have a bike shop inspect your camper's bike and fill out the bike safety check form. WashCo Bikes (137 NE 3rd Ave, Hillsboro) does this check for free up to one week before camp. Other shops' policies vary. For a bike bought after March 1 of the camp year, a copy of the receipt works instead. Bikes from WashCo Bikes don't need the form.

You can also pay by check, in person or by mail: WashCo Bikes / Camp, 137 NE 3rd Ave, Hillsboro, OR 97124.

### Refunds

* 60 days or more before camp: full refund minus a 15% fee
* 31–59 days before camp: 50% refund
* 30 days or less: no refund

<h2 id="faq">Camps FAQ</h2>

### What do the kids do at camp?

A typical day begins at 9 AM when parents drop off campers. Once checked in, kids and instructors meet in the classroom to plan the day.

On Monday, instructors evaluate each camper's riding skills, work on safe-riding drills, and ride to a park for lunch. The rest of the week, campers practice drills, plan where they're going, and head out on an adventure. They spend the better part of each day riding and exploring, and they learn how to take their bikes on transit. Toward the end of the day, they ride back to base camp to meet parents for pick-up.

Friday includes a wrap-up after lunch, with a short quiz so we can see what campers learned (for our purposes only). Every camper gets a goody bag and a letter of completion.

### Can parents ride with their children?

During camp, riding is just for campers and instructors. That time on their own is a big part of how kids build skills and confidence. But we'd love for you to ride with us at the end! Parents and grandparents are invited to a Family Ride on the last day of camp, where your camper can show off what they learned during the week.

On Friday around midday, meet us at the camp base with a bike, helmet, and water bottle. The kids plan the route, so be ready for an adventure. Along the way they may stop to explain why they do certain things, like riding single file instead of four or five abreast; feel free to ask questions. Allow about an hour for the ride. When we get back, we'll hold a short graduation ceremony.

### Do I have to bring my child's bike to camp every day?

No. Bikes, helmets, and locks are stored overnight in a secured classroom.

### I don't get off work until after 3 PM. What happens to my child?

All campers need to be picked up between 3:00 and 3:30 PM. Instructors can sometimes make special arrangements, but if you'll be late, please arrange for someone else to pick up your camper.

During registration you'll list everyone authorized to pick up your child. **If someone not on that list comes for your child, we will not release them.** Let the instructors know about any changes.

### My family is going on vacation during camp week. Is there a partial-week rate?

We don't offer a partial-week rate. Each day builds on the last, and campers get the most out of camp, and out of the group, when they're there all week. Spots are limited, so we ask families to choose a week their camper can attend in full.

### My child doesn't feel well. Should they attend camp?

That's your decision as a parent. Keep in mind that campers ride a lot each day. The first three days build skills and work them into riding, and missing those days makes it hard to keep up on the last two days, when campers ride for most of the day.

### Are there scholarships?

Yes. The number of scholarships we offer varies from year to year and depends on sponsor generosity. To be considered, be referred by an agency or email [camps@washcobikes.org](mailto:camps@washcobikes.org).
