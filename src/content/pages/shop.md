## WashCo Community Bike Center

Visit our shop today for your biking needs! The WashCo Bikes Cycling Center and Community Shop is growing! If you haven't visited in a while, please stop in and look around.

* An expanded, two storefront center!
* Separate showroom full of new, used and completely refurbished bicycles.
* Full-service repair shop offering everything from flat repairs to full tune up at 15-30% less than other shops. See our [Service and Repair Rates](/shop/rates)
* Full retail shop of bike accessories, clothing, bike racks, trailers, car racks, safety gear. If we don't have an accessory you need, we will order it for you.Lots of good, used parts for you to look through if you choose to do repairs yourself.
* We are an official distribution center for OHSU and Think First sponsored bicycle helmets. Retail, needs-based sliding scale and free helmet kiosk.

You'll see friendly faces ready to help you get rolling. We have a team of great mechanics who know bikes well.

We're here to provide a good quality bicycle and repairs at an affordable cost. Stop in and say hi!
