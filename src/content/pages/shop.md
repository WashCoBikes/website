Visit our shop today for your biking needs! The WashCo Bikes Cycling Center and Community Shop is growing! If you haven't visited in a while, please stop in and look around.

* A showroom full of new, used, and fully refurbished bikes.
* Full-service repairs, from flat fixes to complete tune-ups, at 15–30% less than other shops. See our [Service and Repair Rates](#rates).
* Accessories, clothing, bike racks, car racks, trailers, and safety gear. If we don't have what you need, we'll order it.
* Bins of good used parts, if you'd rather do the repair yourself.
* Helmets through OHSU and ThinkFirst: for sale, on a sliding scale, or free from our helmet kiosk.

You'll see friendly faces ready to help you get rolling. We have a team of great mechanics who know bikes well.

We're here to provide a good quality bicycle and repairs at an affordable cost. Stop in and say hi!
