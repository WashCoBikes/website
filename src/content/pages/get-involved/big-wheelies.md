## Be a Big Wheelie!

Big Wheelies help ready bicycles to be given away!

Donations go to the cleaning, refurbishing and transporting of bicycle to be given away.  
Each month a themed cutout with each donor's name on it is pinned to the ceiling of our Community Bike Shop.  
Your support is greatly appreciated.
