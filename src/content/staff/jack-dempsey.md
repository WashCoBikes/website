My connection to bicycling began on a banana-seat Schwinn as a pre-teen, when a bike meant freedom, independence, and the chance to explore. Over the years, bicycling stayed with me in different ways—from commuting to work while living in Hawaii, to recreational riding in Guam, and bike touring in beautiful places like Italy, Japan, and Spain.

Since retiring, I’ve rediscovered just how much joy bicycling brings to my life. What excites me about WashCo Bikes is the opportunity to help more families experience that same sense of freedom, adventure, and fun that biking has given me over the years.

I believe bikes have a special way of bringing people together and creating lasting memories, and I’m grateful for the opportunity WashCo Bikes gives me to support that in our community.
